## What Are Redirect Links?

A redirect link is a URL that automatically sends visitors to a different destination URL. Instead of sharing `https://your-offer.com/product?ref=affiliate123`, you share a shorter, cleaner link like `https://guard.bot/go/summer-sale`.

When someone clicks the redirect link, the server processes the request and sends them to the final destination—all in milliseconds.

**Why marketers use redirect links:**

- **Clean URLs** — Hide long, ugly affiliate parameters
- **Tracking** — Monitor clicks, conversions, and traffic sources
- **Flexibility** — Change the destination without updating ads
- **Branding** — Use your own domain for a professional look
- **A/B Testing** — Split traffic between different landing pages

But here's the problem: every bot on the internet can follow these links too.

Traditional solutions like CAPTCHAs frustrate real customers. IP blocking is a game of whip-a-mole. You need something smarter.

## How Bot Protection Works with Redirect Links

Modern bot protection analyzes every click before allowing access to your destination. Here's what happens in the milliseconds between click and redirect:

### 1. Fingerprint Analysis

The system examines the visitor's browser fingerprint—hundreds of signals including:

- Browser type and version
- Screen resolution and color depth
- Installed fonts and plugins
- WebGL renderer information
- Time zone and language settings

Real browsers have consistent, human-like fingerprints. Bots and headless browsers have telltale inconsistencies.

### 2. Behavioral Signals

How did the visitor arrive? Legitimate users:

- Come from recognizable referrers
- Have realistic mouse movements
- Load pages at human speeds
- Don't make thousands of requests per second

Automated traffic fails these behavioral checks.

### 3. IP Intelligence

The system checks the visitor's IP against known threat databases:

- Datacenter IPs (where bots live)
- Known proxy and VPN ranges
- Previously flagged bad actors
- Geographic anomalies

### 4. Real-Time Decision

In under 100 milliseconds, the system decides: let them through, challenge them, or block them entirely.

**Real humans never notice.** They click, they arrive, they convert. Bots get stopped at the gate.
