Shared vs. own domains

A domain added here is available to every user as a deploy target, as long as the toggle on the shared domains page is on.

Users can still add their own domains under their Domains page — those stay private to them and are always available regardless of this setting.

Adding a domain from your own user section adds it as a personal domain, not a shared one.
