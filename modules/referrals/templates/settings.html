About Referrals

The referral system allows users to earn commission when their referrals pay for subscriptions.

  • Level 1: Direct referrals (someone who used the user's link)
  • Level 2: Referrals brought in by Level 1 users
  • Level 3: Referrals brought in by Level 2 users

Commissions are credited instantly to user balances and can be used for subscriptions or withdrawals.
