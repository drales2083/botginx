How It Works

Whitelist: Only visitors from selected countries/devices are allowed.

Blacklist: Visitors from selected countries/devices are blocked.

Bot Protection: Blocks known bots, Tor, VPNs, datacenters, and headless browsers. Enabled by default.

Behavior Score

Every visitor gets a behavior score from 0 to 100 based on how human-like they act.

What affects the score:

  • Mouse movements and clicks
  • Scroll patterns
  • Time on page
  • Browser fingerprint

Recommended thresholds:

  • 0 - Disabled (default)
  • 20-30 - Block obvious bots
  • 40-50 - Moderate filtering
  • 60+ - Strict (may block some real users)
